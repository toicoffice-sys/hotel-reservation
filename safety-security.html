Safety and Security Policy

Dear Guests,

Here at Chez Rafael the safety and security of our guests, employees, and property are of paramount importance. This policy outlines the procedures and guidelines to ensure a safe and secure environment for all.

Valuables

Guests are strongly advised to keep valuables secure and not leave them unattended in the room. It is recommended to bring valuables with you. Chez Rafael is not liable for items left in or taken from the room.

Public Places

If unfamiliar about the place that you intend to visit, you may inquire at the Front Desk Area. We can give assistance in looking for a transportation or provide a location map.

Room Key

You may keep the room key with you, or you may deposit it at the Front Desk Area every time you go out of the hotel premises. If you had misplaced the room key, please contact us immediately.

CCTV Cameras

For your safety, be aware that the CCTV cameras are in operation in public areas of Chez Rafael.

Room Number of Registered Guests

To ensure safety and security, the room number, company name or address, vehicle plate number and full name of registered guests are shared with the security department to monitor who enters and exits the hotel.

Lost and Found Policy

Chez Rafael is not responsible for any item left behind by a guest. If you discover that you have left behind something of value to you, please let us know immediately and we will try to assist you in locating your lost item.

What to Do in Case of Fire

Preparation
  • Upon check-in, familiarize yourself with emergency exits, safety features, and the fire exit plan posted on the back of your room door.
  • Locate fire exits, alarms, extinguishers, fire hoses, and know how to turn off your air conditioner.
  • Always keep your room key with you and inform hotel staff if you have any disabilities.
Action

If you discover a fire:

  • Keep calm and don't panic, break the nearest fire alarm, call the emergency hotline to notify the fire's location.
  • Do not pack your belongings; prioritize your safety and take your key.
  • Close doors behind you and use the nearest exit staircase, staying low to avoid smoke.
  • Never use the elevator during a fire.
  • If your exit is blocked, return to your room, check the door's condition, and signal for help.
Remember
  • If caught in smoke, stay calm, crawl low to the floor to escape, and avoid jumping from the building.
  • Know the hotel's emergency number and follow the escape plan on your room's entry door to increase survival chances.
  • Do not re-enter the hotel until it is declared safe by hotel management after the fire is out.

Should you need to report an incident or require further assistance, please do not hesitate to call us at the Front Desk Area (local 1618) or Security Department (local 1000).
