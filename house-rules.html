House Rules

What guests need to know before and during their stay at DLSL Chez Rafael.

House Rules

Dear Guests,

We are delighted to have you as guests at Chez Rafael and we want to ensure that your stay is comfortable and enjoyable. To make the experience as pleasant as possible, we kindly request you to familiarize yourself with our hotel rules. Thank you for your cooperation!

Guest Behaviour

Guests are expected to treat others with respect and courtesy, avoiding disruptive or abusive behavior. Please be mindful of others, especially in group settings. Always maintain proper conduct and do not leave valuables unattended.

Use of Facilities

Guests should use hotel rooms and facilities with care and coordinate any room setup changes with hotel staff. Personal belongings should not be displayed near windows to maintain the hotel's exterior appearance. Please report any malfunctions or breakages to our friendly staff immediately. Guests will be held responsible for any damages incurred during their stay.

No Smoking and No Liquor Policy

We take pride in providing a smoke and alcohol-free environment throughout our hotel, as we prioritize the comfort and well-being of all our guests. Smoking and drinking alcohol are strictly prohibited in all areas.

Visitors' Policy

For safety and security, only registered guests are allowed in hotel rooms and to stay overnight. If you wish to have visitors, please inform hotel staff and meet them in designated public areas. Pets are not allowed.

Cleaning

At Chez Rafael we prioritize cleanliness and comfort, with daily room cleaning provided by our housekeeping team. Towels are replaced if placed on the bathroom floor, and bed linens are changed every fifth day unless otherwise requested. For privacy, you may use the Do Not Disturb sign, but we reserve the right to enter your room every three days for security and cleanliness checks. Our staff is always available to assist with additional amenities.

Chez Rafael as a Hotel Laboratory

Chez Rafael is primarily used by CIHTM student trainees who are gaining hands-on experience in the hospitality industry. While they are supervised by professionals, we ask for your understanding and patience with any service differences. Your support helps them develop essential skills.

We greatly appreciate your support in helping them develop the skills they need to succeed.

Thank You!

We appreciate your cooperation in following the hotel rules, which are designed to ensure a safe, secure, and pleasant stay for all guests. Thank you for choosing our Chez Rafael — we look forward to making your stay memorable and enjoyable.

Animo La Salle!
