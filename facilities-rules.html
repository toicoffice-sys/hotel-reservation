Facilities Rules and Regulations

Guidelines for using the rooms, venues, and shared spaces at DLSL Chez Rafael.

Facilities Rules and Regulations

Event Hours: The Client will be granted access to the venue starting at the time specified on the event information only.

Event Set-Up and Clean-Up

  • The Client is responsible for requesting or providing any additional seating, backdrop, platform, podium, or any other items needed for their event.
  • The Client or the contracted catering service provider is responsible for setting up and cleaning up the venue within the agreed upon hours.
  • The Client shall remove all personal property, trash, and other items that were not present when the Client took control of it.

Venue Rules

The Client agrees to adhere to all venue rules and regulations:

  • Usage of all Chez Rafael venues shall be until 8:00 PM only.
  • De La Salle Lipa is strictly implementing No Smoking and No Liquor Policy.
  • Pets are not permitted at Chez Rafael premises.
  • Occupancy limits should be followed to ensure safety (Café 80 pax / Function 40 pax).
  • As part of De La Salle Lipa and Chez Rafael's sustainability program, guests are prohibited from bringing in PET bottles, Styrofoam, and plastic packaging.
  • All volume levels of sound systems must be respectful to others. We suggest borrowing from MHUB.
  • Guests should be aware of the nearest fire exits, alarms, fire extinguishers, and fire hoses.
  • The Client is responsible for any damage to the venue or its contents.
  • Maintenance/Housekeeping staff shall be present during the event to uphold cleanliness and regulatory guidelines.
  • The Client agrees to settle the overtime pay of the Maintenance/Housekeeper present at the event.
  • One Maintenance/Housekeeping staff should be present for events with 40 or fewer guests, and 2 staff for 41 to 80 guests.
  • Guests are requested to be sensitive to others. Proper behavior and courtesy must always be observed.
